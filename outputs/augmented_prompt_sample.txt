OBSERVATION
----------------------------------------------------------------------
assemble_context() never partially includes a chunk - each chunk either fits whole inside the remaining budget or is dropped entirely, so the model never sees a sentence cut off mid-way with a source marker attached to it. Highest-ranked chunks are tried first, so when the budget is tight, the chunks that get dropped are always the least relevant ones already at the bottom of the ranked list. Reserving tokens for instructions, the question, and the answer up front (not just for context) is what keeps the whole request inside the model's context window, not just the retrieved-chunks portion of it.
