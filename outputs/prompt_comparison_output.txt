Hello! I can certainly help clarify the difference between comprehensive and third-party car insurance.

*   **Third-Party Car Insurance:** This coverage protects you against financial liability for damage or injury you cause to *another person or their property* in an accident. It's often the minimum legal requirement.
*   **Comprehensive Car Insurance:** This offers much broader protection. It includes third-party liability coverage *plus* coverage for damage to *your own vehicle* resulting from incidents like collisions, theft, fire, vandalism, or natural disasters.

Comprehensive insurance provides more extensive protection for your vehicle and peace of mind.

*   **Third-Party Car Insurance:** This is the minimum legal requirement in many places. It covers damages or injuries you cause to other people or their property in an accident. It **does not** cover damages to your own vehicle.
