# Recommended Model Settings for a Grounded RAG Assistant

## Recommended Settings

- Temperature: 0.2
- Max Output Tokens: 40
- Top P: 0.3

## Why These Settings?

- Low temperature produces more consistent and factual responses.
- Max output tokens keeps responses concise and controls cost.
- Top P limits token selection to the most likely words, improving reliability.

These settings are appropriate for a RAG assistant because they prioritize accurate, grounded, and consistent responses over creativity.
