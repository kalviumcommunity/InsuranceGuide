SECTION 1: COVERAGE OVERVIEW

This policy provides coverage for dwelling, personal property, and liability protection subject to the terms below. The insured’s dwelling is covered against fire, windstorm, and theft as detailed in Section 3.

Deductibles apply per occurrence and are listed in the Schedule of Coverage. The insurer’s obligation to pay is limited to the policy limits stated on the declarations page.

SECTION 2: EXCLUSIONS

This policy does not cover damage caused by flood, earth movement, or intentional acts by the insured. Acts of God such as earthquakes require separate endorsement coverage.

SECTION 3: CLAIMS PROCESS

To file a claim, the policyholder must notify the insurer within 30 days of the loss event. Failure to report promptly may result in claim denial — see Section 5 for appeal procedures.

SECTION 4: DEFINITIONS

"Dwelling" means the primary structure described on the declarations page. "Insured" means the named policyholder and resident relatives.
